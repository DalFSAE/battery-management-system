# Improvements to be made

- Smaller PCB size, distributed per cell group (flex PCB or small board)
- PCB must fit within the enclosure and be removable for inspection.
- No risk of short circuit to neighboring cells (adequate spacing, insulation, or conformal coating).
- No SMD pin headers that can break — use locking board-to-board or flex connectors.
- Add dedicated positive and negative connectors for high-voltage measurement reference.
- Must use flex PCBs for voltage and temperature sensing connections.
